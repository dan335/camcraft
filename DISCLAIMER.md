## Its programs can be wrong

A toolpath, a simulation, a check that passes or a G-code program from camcraft
can be wrong. A wrong program can crash the machine, break tools, ruin the part
or hurt someone. camcraft's own checks catch some mistakes and miss others:
[PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) lists what the simulator cannot check for you.

Before a program runs, a person who knows the machine, the tooling and the work
holding must check it: in a simulator they trust, then as an air cut above the
stock, with a hand near the e-stop. Whoever runs the program is responsible for
what it does, for what is made with it, and for keeping to the laws and safety
rules where it runs.
